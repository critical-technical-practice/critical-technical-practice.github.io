> **Critical technical practice** is a [critical theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_theory) based approach towards technological design proposed by [Phil Agre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Agre) where [critical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_theory) and [cultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_studies) theories are brought to bear in the work of designers and [engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer). One of the goals of critical technical practice is to increase awareness and critical reflection on the hidden assumptions, ideologies and values underlying technology design. It was developed by Agre in response to a perceived lack in [Artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) research in the late 20th century, and continues to influence critical AI in 2021.
