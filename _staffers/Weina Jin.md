I am a Computer Science Ph.D. Candidate at the Medical Image Analysis Lab, Simon Fraser University. I am supervised by Prof. Ghassan Hamarneh. My research focuses on explainable AI for medical image analysis. 

In my research, I found that despite the fact that explainability is [one of the five AI ethics principles](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11023-018-9482-5), the use of the common evaluation criterion of explainable AI —- plausibility —- can [mislead and manipulate users](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17707). If the sentinel field of ethical AI has such a serious problem, how we can be sure that other technical fields do not have similar problems? Why does a technical field for ethical purposes end up in unethical outcomes? From this counterintuitive phenomenon, I began to [explore the root causes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10588). And critical technical practice is the systematic approach I want to explore to intervene in the roots.
